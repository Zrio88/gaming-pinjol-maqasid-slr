single reviewer. Every stage decision and exclusion reason (E1–E9) is recorded
below. The machine-readable version of these counts is

## Reviewer calibration

Screening was performed by a single reviewer. Inter-reviewer calibration and
Cohen's κ (target ≥ 0.80) could not be performed because no second human
reviewer was available; κ is therefore not reported, and this is declared as a
methodological limitation of the review. This log is the audit trail.
