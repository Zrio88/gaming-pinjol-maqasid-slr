# Protocol appendix (Appendix C) — review protocol, v1.0

This file documents the **pre-specified protocol** (v1.0) for the systematic
review. It was written in advance of screening and is informed by PRISMA-P
(Moher et al., 2015). It fixes, before any record was screened:

- the review questions (RQ1–RQ4);
- the PICOC / PICOS framework;
- the eligibility criteria and the exclusion codes E1–E9;
- the information sources and the draft search strategy;
- the two-stage selection process;
- the data-extraction plan and codebook;
- the quality-appraisal and certainty-appraisal plans; and
- the synthesis plan.

Registration with the **OSF Registries** is planned (alternative: PROSPERO);
**no registration number has been issued** and none is claimed.

## 1. Review questions

- **RQ1.** What is the direction, magnitude, and consistency of the association
  between online gaming behaviour (intensity and in-game monetisation) and
  lending / indebtedness outcomes, and how much of the assumed
  "gaming → pinjol" pathway is direct rather than mediated?
- **RQ2.** Which mechanisms, mediators, and moderators explain the pathway, and
  how do they condition its strength?
- **RQ3.** How are these mechanisms appraised within Islamic commercial ethics
  (israf/tabzīr, maysir, gharar, riba) and against the maqāṣid al-Sharīʿah?
- **RQ4.** What evidentiary gaps remain, and what study designs and policy
  instruments would close them?

## 2. PICOC / PICOS framework

Eligibility was defined by **PICOC** and operationalised through **PICOS**.
The element-by-element specification is in `../data/eligibility-criteria.csv`.

## 3. Eligibility criteria and exclusion codes

The operational inclusion criteria and the full E1–E9 exclusion codes are in
`inclusion-exclusion-codes.md`. Publication window: **2015–2026**, in
Indonesian and English, with a minority of foundational pre-2021 works retained
by citation chaining (≥80% of included scholarly sources dated ≥2021).

## 4. Information sources and search strategy

Databases: **Google Scholar** (first ten pages per string), **Garuda**,
**DOAJ**, **PubMed**, supplemented by hand-searching of core journal tables of
contents and backward/forward citation chaining. Documented retrieval date:
**12 October 2026** (including a documented update/bridge scan on that date).

Concept blocks: GAME × MONETISATION × DEBT × SHARIAH. The verbatim strings per
database are recorded in **Appendix A** (`../search/search-strategies.md`).

**Transparency note.** Scopus and Web of Science were not subscribed at the
authors' institution and were substituted by the databases above.

## 5. Selection process

Two stages: **title–abstract**, then **full text**. Screening was performed by a
**single reviewer**; inter-reviewer calibration and Cohen's κ (target ≥ 0.80)
could not be performed because no second human reviewer was available, and κ is
therefore not reported. Every stage decision and exclusion reason (E1–E9) is
preserved in **Appendix E** (`../search/screening-log.md`); the flow of records
is reported in Figure 1 (PRISMA 2020 flow diagram).

## 6. Data extraction

A standardised form guided extraction into the evidence table (**Appendix B**,
`../data/included-studies.csv`). Missing data are recorded as `NR` (not
reported) and were not imputed. The field list is the extraction codebook
(`codebook.md`).

## 7. Quality appraisal

Methodological quality was appraised with the **MMAT 2018** (Hong et al., 2018)
for empirical studies and the **JBI Text and Opinion checklist** for
legal-normative scholarship, reported at the overall level as high, medium, or
low. A sensitivity analysis re-examined whether the principal conclusions held
when low-quality studies were excluded from the thematic narrative.

## 8. Synthesis plan

Synthesis was narrative, following **SWiM** (Campbell et al., 2020), and
thematic (Braun & Clarke, 2006). Because comparable effect sizes for pinjol
outcomes are scarce (predominantly `NR`), **no quantitative meta-analysis** was
performed; a harvest table of findings was constructed instead. A priori
subgroups: (a) international vs Indonesian context; (b) behavioural vs financial
outcomes; (c) game type; and (d) the strict separation of online gaming (core)
from online gambling (comparator).

## 9. Certainty appraisal

Confidence in each theme was graded with **GRADE-CERQual** (Lewin et al., 2018)
across four components — methodological limitations, coherence, adequacy of
data, and relevance — yielding a rating of high / moderate / low / very low per
finding (`../data/certainty-of-evidence.csv`).

## 10. Amendments after protocol v1.0

No post-hoc change was made to the review questions or the eligibility
criteria. The single deviation from the plan is the **inability to perform dual
screening / κ** (single reviewer, §5 above), which is declared as a
methodological limitation in the manuscript (§2.4, §4.4).
