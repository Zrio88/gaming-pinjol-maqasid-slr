# Appendix D — Completed PRISMA 2020 checklist

**Review title:** Monetised Play and Indebted Borrowing: Online Gaming and the Rise of Online Lending (Pinjol) in Indonesia — A PRISMA 2020 Systematic Review Through the Maqāṣid al-Sharīʿah

**Reporting guideline:** PRISMA 2020 (Page et al., 2021), *BMJ*, 372, n71.

The checklist below follows the 27 items of the PRISMA 2020 statement. The
**Location** column refers to sections of the manuscript and to files in this
repository. `NR` = not reported in the extracted source; `n/a` = not applicable
to this review's design.

## Notes

- Items 10 (`Data items`), 13 (`Synthesis methods`), 16 (`Study selection`),
  20 (`Results of syntheses`), and 23–24 (`Discussion`, `Registration and
  protocol`) are each split into sub-items (a, b, …) in PRISMA 2020 and are
  shown here at that granularity.
- Where a PRISMA element was genuinely not performed (`n/a`/not assessed), the
  checklist states the omission and the reason rather than marking it compliant.
