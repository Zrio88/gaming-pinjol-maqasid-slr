# Data availability statement

Suggested wording for the manuscript (declarations section):

> **Data availability.** The full research-data package — the review protocol,
> verbatim database search strings, the screening and exclusion log, and the
> 36-study extraction table (S01–S36) with quality and certainty appraisals —
> is openly available at https://github.com/Zrio88/gaming-pinjol-maqasid-slr.

## Notes

- The repository is public and contains no author-identifying information, so
  the link is safe to include during double-anonymized peer review.
- On acceptance, the corresponding article's DOI and author details should be
  added to `CITATION.cff` and to the citation block in `README.md`.
